About this resource hub

This collection is intended as a practical starting point for people creating, evaluating, researching or commissioning immersive technologies. It brings together external guidance, standards work, developer resources, tools and evidence relevant to accessibility in virtual, augmented and mixed reality.

Inclusion in the library does not imply endorsement by the working group. Resources should be reviewed in context and alongside direct involvement of disabled people.
